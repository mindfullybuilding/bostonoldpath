# Design QA

Implemented against the approved MCGB redesign plan, the supplied meeting screenshots, existing MCGB source, and the CZC comparison audit. This is a blended redesign rather than a pixel clone of CZC.

Final user steering takes precedence over the initial plan:

- H1 is exactly “A deep bow to you, dear friend.” The added “Mindfulness, practiced together.” heading is removed.
- Shared top bar is butter yellow #F4DF92; its Find a group button is white. Other primary page actions remain yellow.
- Compact navigation uses the traditional hamburger glyph, with accessible name “Open menu”.
- Decorative AI filler and redundant microcopy were removed; DESIGN.md prohibits it globally.
- Current remaining draft copy may be revised by the user after release.
- Public agenda and subscriptions are primary. Google is an optional signed-in view, following anonymous-access testing and the user’s decision.

The supplied Boston flag palette is documented as a reference in DESIGN.md, not adopted as a claim of official city branding. Licensed lineage photographs are credited and accurately identified as Plum Village/Thích Nhất Hạnh, rather than local Boston photographs.

Visual verification covers content-driven hero height, spacious 48px desktop dropdown rows, 56px drawer links, 44px controls, readable metadata, stacked phone layouts, reading measures, and responsive content without fixed-height text containers. Eight viewport widths and genuine 200% text enlargement were checked automatically. Desktop and compact screenshots are preserved alongside machine-readable evidence.

No new community contributions, event listings, quotes, or venue access guarantees were invented. The historical newsletter archive seeds Community Voices. Group source records and facilitator reading copy remain available. Known accessibility limitations are listed separately in ACCESSIBILITY-VERIFICATION.md.
