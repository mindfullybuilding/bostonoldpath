# MCGB design system

This is the normative contract for all current MCGB HTML pages. Shared tokens live in `public/assets/mcgb.css`; shared layout is generated by `scripts/build-site.mjs`. Change this document and the shared implementation together. The dated archive is intentionally unchanged.

## Purpose and hierarchy

Welcome people into Greater Boston practice communities in the Plum Village tradition. The primary action is **Find a group**. New here, Events, Resources, Our community, and Mailing list retain the same order across desktop and mobile. Events leads to the finder’s independent community calendar. Community Voices is a public editorial collection with a separately configured submission link.

## Tokens

Colors: ivory #fefbf3, white #ffffff, ink #222222, sun #ffd22a, butter #f4df92, earth #8b572a, moss #4f6f52, muted #625f58. Text uses ink on ivory/white; yellow controls use ink. Earth is the focus/interactive color. Never use pale borders as the only control boundary or yellow as text on white.

Typography: Georgia editorial headings; system sans serif body/controls. Body 18px desktop, 16px below 900px, line-height 1.6. H1 fluid 36–64px; H2 32–44px; H3 22–28px. Navigation and controls stay at least 16px. Secondary text stays at least 14px. Reading measure 65ch.

Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80px. Content max 1160px. Gutters 24px, reducing to 16px below 600px. Standard sections 80px desktop/48px mobile. Cards use 24px padding; compact directory metadata uses 16px. Radius 8px, editorial imagery 12px. Borders 1px; focus outline 3px with 4px offset.

## Layout and components

- Header: 80px desktop; 72px compact. Full navigation starts at 1200px. Dropdown links have 48px minimum rows, left alignment, explicit disclosure buttons, overview links, and Escape/outside dismissal.
- Mobile drawer: the compact header uses the traditional hamburger glyph (☰), with accessible name “Open menu”, instead of visible “Menu” text. Right aligned, maximum 384px/full viewport on narrow phones; 24px padding, 56px link rows, visible close button, native modal background isolation, focus restoration. Close safely when switching to desktop.
- Primary controls: at least 44px high. One yellow primary action per action group; secondary actions are outlined or underlined. Text links are underlined in prose. Selected filters include native checked state and text, not color alone.
- Homepage: text and image columns above 900px; stacked below. Hero is content-driven, never a fixed viewport with clipped overflow. Welcome, participation, community voices, events, resources, connection remain visible in document flow.
- Directory: native form controls and readable metadata; filter results announce counts. Details include contact and directions. A map is supplementary, clearly a regional overview independent of filters.
- Calendar: independent of directory filters. Accessible agenda is always present. Google embed is supplementary and requires sign-in; it is disclosed on request at every width. The verified public website agenda is primary. Loading, empty, error and refresh-failure states must be distinct.
- Reading pages: 65ch body, open sections and rules rather than repeated nested cards. Existing practice-history and facilitator structures may use compact subsection spacing (32px), never bespoke body sizes.
- Resource library: static links and native disclosures preserve deep anchors and work without JavaScript. Disclosures use 48px summary targets.
- Dialog: maximum 720px, internal scrolling bounded by viewport, visible close, meaningful label, focus restoration, Escape. No essential action appears exclusively behind hover.
- Archive: newsletter preview images are supplementary to real text titles and labeled historical PDFs. Do not claim PDF accessibility certification.

## Accessibility and content

WCAG 2.2 AA is the engineering target, not an automatic certification claim. Verify complete states, keyboard, real assistive technology where available, contrast, 200% text enlargement, 400% reflow and text-spacing overrides. Never mark untested checks passed. Honor reduced motion. Reserve image dimensions; provide meaningful alt text and real captions. Caption Plum Village images as Plum Village, not Boston. Record image source, author, license and modifications in photo credits.

All internal pages have one H1, a skip link, main landmark, consistent navigation, footer contact and accessibility information. Do not invent events, testimonials, participation guarantees, venue accessibility, or publication consent. Submitted work is reviewed manually.

## Verification targets

320, 390, 430, 768, 820, 1024, 1280, 1440px; short landscape screens; open drawers/dropdowns/dialogs; long labels; no results; failed feed/clipboard; deep links. All essential first-party content must remain reachable without horizontal page scrolling. New components require an entry here; exceptions require a named reason.

## Named exceptions

The compact header retains 16px control text at every width; its brand is MCGB. Footer focus uses sun on ink. Directory cards use 24px padding and 16px metadata. Calendar auxiliary labels use 16px. Photo credits and archival provenance may use 14px. A native filter disclosure replaces the desktop sidebar below 900px. Phone drawer padding reduces to 16px below 600px.

## Editorial rules: no generic AI filler

Use direct, specific language that explains the community, a destination, or an action. Do not add decorative reassurance, invented mottos, or generic AI microcopy to fill space. Banned examples include “In person and online. At your own pace.”, “Space to pause. Space to begin again.”, “Keep a thread of connection”, and similar paired sentence fragments. Do not add “at your own pace”, “come as you are”, or unsupported welcome/access promises as stock interface copy.

Use factual labels such as “Upcoming community events”, “Join our mailing lists”, “Group details & contact”, and “Preparing for a first visit”. Supporting text must add information a visitor needs. Remove it when the heading or action already communicates the point. The homepage H1 is exactly “A deep bow to you, dear friend.” Do not add “Mindfulness, practiced together.” or a second welcome slogan. The chosen greeting is not a license to invent additional slogans. Apply this rule to every page, caption, status, component, and future addition.

## Header color treatment

The shared top bar uses butter yellow (#f4df92), with ink text and a white Find a group button. The primary action remains yellow on ivory/white page surfaces. Mobile and desktop share this treatment. Do not use yellow text on white.
