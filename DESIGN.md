# MCGB design system

This is the authoritative contract for current HTML pages. Shared layouts are generated by `scripts/build-site.mjs`, tokens/components live in `public/assets/mcgb.css`, and interactions in `public/assets/mcgb.js`. Update these together. The dated pre-migration snapshot is immutable.

## Purpose and reference hierarchy

Help people learn about the practice, find a sangha, contact an organizer, read community material, and receive announcements. References, in order:

1. Approved wording and tone in `outputs/photos-transcription/review.html` and its document-text companions. These files remain local and excluded from Git/deployment. Preserve the attribution and Peaceful Garden context; do not turn one sangha’s schedule, donations, or access arrangements into promises about every group.
2. The last pre-navigation draft, preserved at tag `mcgb-pre-redesign-2026-10-19` and `design-prototypes/archive/2026-10-19-live/`. Retain its direct language, standalone pages, explicit directory metadata, and filter/list/map composition. Its mock history, dead links, broken calendar and hidden content are not approved production behavior.
3. CZC’s clear destination labels, real photographs, and restrained content surfaces. Do not copy its compressed typography, oversized photo height, hover-dependent menus, ornamental timeline or empty social feed.

The audit contract was recorded before refinement in `outputs/refinement-audit-2026-10-19/`; the versioned report records decisions and verification.

## Navigation and page responsibilities

Main destinations, in the same order on desktop and mobile: **Our practice / Our community / Events / Resources / Mailing lists / Find a group**. Find a group remains the prominent header action. Home is the brand link.

Use direct links. There are no desktop dropdown menus: the short hierarchy does not justify an extra menu layer or arrows. Never put the same destination in multiple top-level categories. Contextual links are allowed when they complete a reader’s current task; duplicating every destination in every section is not.

Community local navigation: **About MCGB / Community Voices / Newsletter archive**. Practice local navigation: **Our practice / New here**. Use `aria-current`. Guide pages have a working Resources → Sangha resources breadcrumb and sibling guide links. Do not flatten guides into a long resources page or copy the archive gallery into Voices. Canonical URLs and legacy resource anchors remain available.

Events intentionally links to `find-a-sangha.html#calendar`, a distinct task within the finder as requested. Find a group links to the directory start. Explain that directory filters do not filter the events calendar. These are purposeful entry points, not separate maintained calendars.

The footer provides organization identity and Contact, Accessibility, Photo credits, Privacy & site information. It does not repeat the full primary menu.

## Color and typography

- Text: black `#000000` and white `#FFFFFF` only. Headings, prose, captions, links and metadata inherit black on light surfaces. Do not use blue body text, gray low-contrast helper text, colored heading scales or gold text.
- Header and primary controls: `#3276C2`, an explicitly defined accessible companion to Continental Blue `#367BCA`. White text on the companion is 4.66:1; exact Continental Blue/white is 4.34:1 and fails ordinary text. Do not silently use the exact swatch with small white navigation labels.
- Header Find a group and hero primary action: white surface, black text. Page primary buttons: blue surface, white text. No gold/butter-yellow interface treatment in this revision.
- Surfaces: white; neutral hover `#F7F7F5`; decorative rules `#D8D8D8`. Controls needing a boundary have black borders. Photography retains its natural colors.
- Focus: 3px black outline / 4px white backing on light surfaces; reverse on blue and hero. Check both boundaries, not only the outline’s nominal color. Respect forced-colors mode.
- Font: system sans serif for site identity, all interface, headings and reading pages. Georgia is reserved for the homepage greeting. No font icons or Unicode approximations for controls.
- Body: 18px desktop; 16px below 900px; line-height 1.65. Lede 20px/18px. H1 32–44px; H2 28px/26px; H3 22px. Directory H1 32px, group names 20px. Navigation/controls at least 16px; factual captions/provenance at least 14px. Use normal wrapping; no fixed-height text containers, arbitrary manual line breaks, or globally balanced headings.

The [user-supplied Boston flag reference](https://www.flagcolorcodes.com/boston) lists Continental Blue, medium/dark blues, gold and white. This is a reference, not an official city branding claim. The companion is an accessibility adjustment, not an additional purported flag color.

## Spacing and layout

Scale: **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64px**. Maximum content width 1160px; reading measure 65ch. Gutters 24px, 16px below 600px. Sections 48px desktop, 40px below 900px. Paragraph gaps 20px; heading-to-body 20px. Consecutive reading subsections 40px. Prefer one rule between substantive sections, not a box around every paragraph.

Header: content-driven, minimum 88px desktop/72px compact; full nav from 1200px. Wrapping remains available under text enlargement. Switch to compact brand, Find a group and a standard 24px menu icon below 1200px. Minimum 44px controls; navigation 48px rows. Short windows use a non-sticky header.

Align comparable columns at their top edges. Do not vertically center a short callout next to a long passage, or a captioned photo next to unrelated copy. Use a grid when destinations are parallel, prose when they are sequential. No decorative numbering of unrelated choices, nested cards, or empty columns added for symmetry.

Components use 4px radius, 1px rules, no ornamental shadows, no palette gradients. Standard card padding 24px, directory 20px/16px phone. Interface text uses weight 600; headings 650. Icon assets are unmodified Lucide 1.59.0 menu/x with ISC license in `public/assets/icons/`; inherit current text color. Accessible names live on controls, icons are hidden from assistive technology.

## Named page/component rules

- **Homepage:** content-sized landscape hero, Georgia greeting exactly “A deep bow to you, dear friend.”, original introductory wording split between hero and introduction, Find a group and New here. Neutral 64% scrim guarantees white contrast over bright pixels; solid black fallback, no parallax/autoplay. Practice/community/resources are three compact text destinations. Community Voices pairs with one real newsletter preview, not a duplicate archive. Events and mailing announcements align at top. Do not add standalone “living tradition”, “continue learning” or repeated newcomer promotional blocks without distinct information.
- **Directory:** preserve the original filter/list/map-calendar arrangement: 180px filters, flexible single-column list, 320px map/calendar rail; 24px gaps. At tablet widths put the rail below in two columns; phone stacks. Filter categories are native disclosures; mobile has a clearly labeled filter toggle and search. Each group shows labeled Day, Time, Location and a visible Contact organizer link. More details includes format, address, access information and actual email; contact remains visible when details are open. Do not replace the list with a generic two-column card catalog.
- **Calendar:** Groups.io is the sole maintained source. Accessible agenda primary; imported Google view is optional, closed, and clearly sign-in dependent at all sizes. Map is supplementary with directory alternatives. No private account checking. Six provider disclosures, a copyable URL, honest refresh timing and failure recovery. Loading, empty, error, partial refresh failure, and loaded results stay distinguishable. On an empty calendar, keep useful subscription routes without inventing events or filling a huge dashboard.
- **Resources:** category anchors and native disclosures, simple text entries. Actual facilitator guides remain standalone pages. No visible dummy resource cards. Unknown builder enrollment/mentoring destinations use an honest MCGB inquiry link, not an invented working form. Preserve old anchors even when simplifying.
- **Voices and archive:** submission instructions belong to Voices; actual editions belong to the archive. Form destination configured once. Do not call the temporary form temporary in reader-facing copy, or claim it accepts uploads. Manual review and permission instructions remain. Historical PDF accessibility limitations and help contact remain on the archive.
- **Reading pages:** 65ch, ordinary paragraphs and subsections. Source wording and attribution take priority over invented slogans. No fabricated historical timeline.
- **Drawer:** native modal dialog from the right, max 384px, 24px/16px padding; 56px rows, standard menu/x icons, safe-area inset, internal scrolling. Keyboard containment, Escape, background isolation, focus restoration and breakpoint focus movement required. No giant duplicate second sitemap.

## Editorial rules: no generic AI filler

Use concrete community language and accurate destination labels. The original draft and supplied transcription guide the tone. Do not silently rewrite approved passages for marketing polish. Record material copy changes and source context in the audit report.

Do not add reassurance fragments, invented mottos, broad inspirational promises or prose describing interface features. Banned examples: “In person and online. At your own pace.”, “Space to pause. Space to begin again.”, “Keep a thread of connection”, “A living tradition” as an empty section label, and similar stock copy. No “at your own pace”, “come as you are”, or unsupported cost, accessibility, welcome, beginner or registration guarantees. Remove a supporting sentence if it adds no information beyond its heading/action. Do not repeat the same destination with slightly different invented action labels.

No second welcome slogan or “Mindfulness, practiced together.” No filler boxes to balance a row. No fake community contributions, testimonials, events, timelines or local photographs. Caption licensed Plum Village photos as France. Preserve sources/licenses/cropping disclosures in Photo credits.

Test complete tasks, not isolated components: main navigation to child pages; Resources to each standalone guide and back; group filters/details/contact; calendar/URL copy/tutorials; newsletter PDF versus mailing subscription; contribution form handoff. Automated scans do not replace manual or real screen-reader evaluation. Mark untested assistive technologies untested. Retain historical PDF/external-process limitations.

Preserve source/tag/previous production URL. Push to GitHub main, explicitly deploy to the existing Vercel project, and verify the public alias. Never deploy local transcription/photo-review or meeting materials. `.gitignore` and `.vercelignore` must exclude these inputs, generated test artifacts, credentials and caches.
