# MCGB design system

This is the authoritative design contract. Shared HTML is generated by `scripts/build-site.mjs`; CSS and interactions live in `public/assets/mcgb.css` and `public/assets/mcgb.js`. Update these together. The pre-migration archive and tag `mcgb-pre-water-gloss-2026-10-19` preserve earlier versions.

## Purpose, sources, and visual direction

Help visitors understand mindfulness, find and contact a sangha, participate in community events, read community work, and receive announcements. Expressive visual design and strong usability belong together. “Human” means intentional composition, actual content, consistent interactions, and source-grounded language. It does not mean flat, sparse, or stripped of personality.

The selected water-gloss screenshot of 19 October 2026 is the visual reference for the header and controls: Continental Blue #367BCA, a curved reflective upper surface, translucent highlights, rounded contours, and a deeper lower edge. This intentionally supersedes earlier blanket bans on gradients, shadows, and illustration. The river is an explicitly requested illustration, not an excuse to add ornamental timelines elsewhere.

Content references, in order: approved copy in the local `outputs/photos-transcription/review.html` and document-text companions; preserved pre-navigation pages; actual MCGB publications and organizer records. Preserve source context and attribution. Peaceful Garden arrangements must not become promises about every sangha. CZC informs clear navigation, not its compressed dropdown spacing or small labels.

## Navigation and page responsibilities

The brand links home. Desktop navigation is **About / Get Involved / Community Voices / Mailing Lists / Find a Group**. The last is the prominent white button.

| Group            | Destinations, in order                                                           |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| About            | Newcomers; Our Practice; Greater Boston Community; Resources                     |
| Get Involved     | Find a Sangha; Events Calendar; Gather with Friends (external Togetherness site) |
| Community Voices | Direct overview page; publications and submission form are presented there       |
| Mailing Lists    | Existing standalone subscription page                                            |
| Find a Group     | Existing sangha directory                                                        |

About and Get Involved use arrow-free disclosure buttons, not ARIA application menus. Click/Enter/Space toggle; Escape closes and restores focus; outside click/focus closes; hover is an additional convenience on fine pointers. Dropdown rows are at least 48px high and left aligned. Maintain a safe pointer path between trigger and panel. Only one opens at once. Mark current pages and parent groups without relying on color alone.

Below 1200px retain the full organization name, Find a Group, and the standard hamburger icon. Never replace the visible name with MCGB. Below 600px use a 13px identity wordmark so the full name, Find a Group and hamburger share one row at default text size. This is a named identity exception; action labels remain at least 16px and touch targets at least 44px. Allow the organization name to wrap naturally, and allow controls to move to a new row when text is enlarged. The right-hand modal drawer uses the same grouped hierarchy and 56px rows. Expandable groups use buttons with explicit expanded state; no arrow glyphs. Support Escape, keyboard containment, background isolation, internal scrolling, safe-area padding, focus restoration, and breakpoint focus movement. No invisible focused controls after resizing.

Use contextual breadcrumbs, not a second competing horizontal menu. Resources → Sangha resources leads to each of the five standalone guides. Preserve canonical URLs and old resource anchors. Events goes to `find-a-sangha.html#calendar`, the same source and presentation as the finder. Gather with Friends goes directly to Togetherness; do not add an intermediary page with no distinct content.

## Color, typography, and surfaces

- Exact base token: **#367BCA**. Layer a dark translucent substrate over the text-bearing region and a pale curved reflection above it. White normal text must meet 4.5:1 against the actual rendered gradient, including all interaction states; base blue alone is insufficient (about 4.34:1). Decorative highlights must not cross label zones. Reading text is black on white; interface text is black or white only. No gold or butter-yellow controls.
- Shared blue gloss: stable darker middle under white labels, top reflection, deeper bottom rim, restrained elevation. Strong depth on header and primary actions; lighter white gloss on secondary buttons and disclosures; white inset input surfaces. Keep prose and images free of heavy gloss overlays.
- White header Find a Group and hero primary button, black text. Selected nav tabs use a darker glossy raised surface with a visible border. Hover/pressed/selected states never rely solely on tiny opacity changes. Disabled controls retain readable text and a semantic disabled state.
- Radii: header 0px (full-width bar), footer 0px (full-width bar), primary/secondary controls 14px, dropdown/drawer surfaces 20px, cards/details/inputs 12px. Do not round every paragraph or turn all links into pills.
- Fonts: system sans serif for identity, interface, headings, and reading pages; Georgia only for the homepage greeting. Body 18px desktop/16px below 900px, line-height 1.65; lede 20/18px; H1 fluid 32–44px (hero 36–62px), H2 28/26px, H3 22px. Controls/nav at least 16px, factual notes at least 14px. No fixed-height text containers or manual heading line breaks.
- Focus: 3px black outline with 4px white backing on light surfaces; reverse on blue/hero. Focus must remain visible over gradients and shadows. Forced colors removes decorative gradients and shadows in favor of system colors/borders. No autoplay, parallax, shimmer, or animated water. Respect reduced motion.

## Spacing and component states

Scale: **4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64px**. Content 1160px maximum; reading measure 65ch. Gutters 24px desktop, 16px phone. Sections 48px desktop/40px mobile. Paragraph and heading/body gaps 20px; reading subsections 40px. Comparable columns align at their top edges. No empty boxes or columns for symmetry.

Header minimum 88px desktop/72px compact, content driven and able to grow for enlarged text. Dropdown rows 48px, drawer rows 56px, all other interactive targets at least 44px. Short windows use a non-sticky header. Cards 24px padding, group records 20px/16px phone. Use a single component rule for every normal/hover/focus/pressed state, with documented contextual white header/hero variants rather than accumulating overrides.

Buttons use a consistent raised contour; text links remain underlined links. Details and filters use a lighter glossy summary with readable open state and native semantics. Inputs use explicit labels, inset white surfaces, and black boundaries. Loading, empty, unavailable, partial refresh failure, and success are separate states with useful next actions. Do not leave decorative or nonfunctional controls visible.

## Named page rules

- **Homepage:** approved greeting exactly “A deep bow to you, dear friend.”, retained landscape and source introduction. Content-driven hero with 64% black scrim and solid fallback. No second slogan. Practice/community routes, real publication preview, events, and mailing connection each have a distinct purpose.
- **Newcomers:** introductory quote exactly “Go as a river” — Thích Nhất Hạnh, in italics. Center the title and quote on desktop to share the river’s axis; align them left below 900px. This replaces the former “You may be curious…” sentence. Decorative illustrated river with five semantic ordered stops: Our Practice; Start Practicing (Plum Village app); Practice with Us (separate sangha/events actions); Gather with Friends; Deepen Your Practice (one Resources action). Desktop river runs down the middle with alternating text; below 900px a wider river is cropped along the left margin and softly masked before a single readable text column. Preserve natural stone proportions; do not stretch a narrow strip. Illustration never dictates text height, reading order, focus order, or scroll position. No hidden content, scroll hijacking, or required sequential completion. Do not append the removed first-visit/access guidance section.
- **Finder:** preserve filter/list/map-calendar structure: 180px filters, flexible single-column records, 320px rail, 24px gaps. Tablet rail below in two columns; phone stacked. Labeled Day/Time/Location, visible organizer contact, more details and physical-address directions remain. Filters apply only to directory records; do not add a map/filter caption.
- **Calendar:** Groups.io remains the sole maintained source. Public first-party month calendar and agenda use the same API; Google Calendar is optional, closed, sign-in dependent. Six provider tutorials and URL-copy fallback live on the standalone subscription page. No account access. Preserve empty/error/retry/partial-refresh behavior. No invented events.
- **Community Voices:** editorial presentation of actual local publications, covers, and verified credits. Poetry zine is New England Buddhist Poetry Exchange, not a claim that all broader Buddhist poetry is MCGB work. Broader Plum Village art/song teaching resources stay in Resources. Do not publish empty art/song sections or invented contributions. The configured form accepts links/descriptions, not file uploads; manual review and permission guidance remain.
- **Newsletter archive:** large cover previews grouped by year, newest first; actual PDF destinations, no JavaScript dependency for browsing. Keep historical-document accessibility limitations and assistance contact. Distinguish reading editions from subscribing to mailing lists.
- **Mailing lists:** restore choosing one/both lists, selected addresses and email actions, then Choose → Send → Confirm guidance, including browser-based email. Preserve exact addresses and Groups.io confirmation requirements. No automatic subscription or false success states.
- **Reading/resources:** preserve substantive copy, source attribution, actual standalone guides and resource anchors. Short intros, credited previews, or meaningful excerpts may complement links; no filler to make a page taller.
- **Footer:** three columns on blue: organization identity, participation links, contact/site information. Stack on phones. White readable text, useful link spacing, no generic closing slogan or dead legal links.

## Editorial rules

Do not silently rewrite approved passages. Identify material changes and their sources in release notes. New newcomer explanations may connect existing resources without inventing guarantees about welcome, cost, registration, accessibility, or suitability.

No generic AI filler: “In person and online. At your own pace.”, “Space to pause”, “Keep a thread of connection”, empty “A living tradition” headings, reassurance fragments, or descriptions of interface features. Remove text that adds nothing beyond its heading/action. No “Mindfulness, practiced together.” No fake events, testimonials, history, contributions, or local photographs. Caption Plum Village as France. Record licenses/crops and generated illustration provenance.

## Verification and release

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, without a certification claim. Check navigation and complete tasks, all redesigned pages and important open/selected/error states. Check 320,390,430,768,820,1024,1280,1440px; short landscape; 200% enlargement, 400% reflow equivalent, spacing overrides, reduced motion, forced colors, image failure, failed feed/clipboard/embeds. Measure gradient contrast at actual label zones in normal/hover/selected states. Real assistive-technology checks not performed must be marked untested; retain third-party/PDF limitations.

Preserve source/tag/previous production URL. Verify local and Vercel preview, push to GitHub main, explicitly deploy to the existing Vercel project, then verify public production. `.gitignore`/`.vercelignore` exclude local reference images, transcription, meeting material, credentials, caches, and generated test output. No public archive link.

## Interface refinement — 19 October 2026

Apply these rules alongside the global component specifications:

- Header and footer are full-width rectangular bars with **zero corner radius**. Preserve their existing gloss, colors, height, spacing, shadows, and content. Rounded buttons and panels remain.
- Homepage secondary action reads **New? Start here.**, styled as a transparent button with a white border and white text, matching the neighboring primary button's dimensions and typography.
- Only About and Get Involved have dropdowns. Community Voices is a direct link with the same hover/current/focus treatment as Mailing Lists, on desktop and in the mobile drawer. Its publication and form links remain on its overview page.
- No standalone “External website”, “External site”, “External publication”, “Optional further reading”, production-source notes, or similar eyebrow fragments. Remove their elements and resulting margins. Necessary document formats, error states, permission requirements and subscription instructions remain.
- Photo and text attributions belong to the central **Credits** page linked from the footer, with authors, sources, licenses and modifications retained. Do not crowd photographs with attribution paragraphs. Required credit placement is governed by each license. Use the wider 2006 Paris portrait by Duc (pixiduc), CC BY-SA 2.0, with the verified Commons source on Credits. Getty images require an appropriate existing license and a licensed file before production use; stock previews and watermarks are not deployable assets. Keep license receipts and originals outside Git and deployment.
- Newcomer stop three is **Practice with Us**. Stop five has one **Resources** button to the resource index; no separate Five Mindfulness Trainings link or optional-reading caption. Remove the former Before joining / gathering format / access section entirely. Retain the centered desktop / left-aligned mobile title and italic river quote.
- Togetherness is presented through Get Involved, the newcomer journey and the footer; remove it from the Greater Boston Community page. Label its footer link **Gather with Friends** without a parenthetical qualifier.
- Our Practice's group-practice explanation draws from Plum Village's international sangha directory: community support, collective practice and maintaining mindfulness together. Paraphrase rather than inventing local attendance or access promises; credit the source centrally.
- The resource index is titled **Resources**, without a generic introductory sentence. Put category links in a bordered, padded **Jump to section** tool. Use consistent blue resource tiles for all real destinations. Collection headings group visible child tiles; do not hide Community Members or Community Builders behind disclosures. Preserve legacy IDs and standalone guide URLs. Unknown destinations remain absent or use the existing genuine inquiry route.
- The finder retains filter/list/map structure. Center the map frame within its section; remove the explanatory filter/map caption. More details and Contact organizer are side-by-side controls, with full-width expanded details below. Keep native disclosure keyboard behavior and no-script availability.
- Show a public month calendar using the same event API, independently of the optional signed-in Google view. Empty months still show dates and an honest empty state; never fabricate events. Month controls operate without moving focus, and date/event counts are understandable without color. The accessible agenda remains available and times include their own EST/EDT indication; remove the separate “Times shown in America/New_York…” sentence.
- **Subscribe to calendar** links to `/subscribe-calendar.html`, containing the existing six-provider tutorials, copyable ICS URL, fallback and removal instructions. Keep old finder subscription hashes working through a compatibility redirect without duplicating the tutorial on the finder.
