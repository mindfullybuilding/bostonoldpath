Together in Greater Boston

Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston

Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston is an interfaith collective learning and practicing mindfulness in the Plum Village tradition of Thích Nhất Hạnh.

Local and online groups

Our sanghas meet across the region and online. Each has its own rhythm, organizers, and ways of gathering. The directory helps you find a place to begin.

We explore how to bring mindfulness into our lives, families, work, and society — throughout the day, as well as during meditation.
