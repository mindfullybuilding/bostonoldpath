Our community

Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston (MCGB) represents a large number of sanghas that practice in the tradition of Plum Village (founded by Thich Nhat Hanh). This includes reading the Dharma, gathering as Sangha, and engaging with our inner Buddha to find presence of mind, and compassion for all beings.

The many Sanghas practice both individually and collectively, with independent Sanghas meeting regularly and practicing in a way fitting their members, and collectively gathering for events such as peaceful walks, ceremonies such as the 5 Mindfulness Trainings transmission, Days of Mindfulness, and groups such as the book club.

While Mindful Communities of Greater Boston is a newer initiative, the community of practice in the Boston region has a great history, including many visits from Thich Nhat Hanh and many members of the Order of Interbeing (an order of ordained lay individuals). Learn more under Resources.
