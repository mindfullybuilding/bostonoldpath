Our mission

The mission of Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston (MCGB) is to nurture compassion and understanding within the sangha and throughout the larger society. Relieving the suffering of all beings is an integral part of our practice.

We come together regularly to meditate and deepen our practice. Through dharma discussion and mindful action, we encourage and nourish one another. Together, we celebrate the joys and wonders of life, while working, each in our own way and together, to relieve suffering in the world.

Our roots

The Boston sangha began sitting together in 1989. Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston is a lay Buddhist community inspired by the teachings of Vietnamese Zen monk, peace activist, and author Thích Nhất Hạnh, as well as by teachers within and outside the Buddhist tradition.

MCGB is part of the Community of Mindful Living, an international fellowship of Buddhist practitioners and social activists. At its heart is the Order of Interbeing, a community of lay practitioners and monastics founded by Thích Nhất Hạnh in the 1960s, during the American–Vietnamese War.
