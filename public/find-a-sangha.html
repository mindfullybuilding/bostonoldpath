Find a Sangha

Sangha offers an opportunity to connect authentically with others and to feel safe enough to look at our own suffering in order to change it. We’ve learned “what we don’t transform, we transmit.” We each take in the Dharma through our own filters and, happily, it manifests in each of us differently.

Peaceful Songbird Sangha

Day
Saturday
Time
9:30-11:30 AM
Location
Roslindale
More Details

We meditate, check-in with one another, share a reading from a teacher of the dharma, and follow with dharma discussion, metta practice and a final meditation. We practice in the Plum Village tradition of Thích Nhất Hạnh, with its emphasis on engaged Buddhism and the art of mindful living. We began meeting at Chua Vietnam, a beautiful Vietnamese temple in Roslindale, MA. We have been practicing online via zoom since March of 2020. Once a month we meet in person, dates depending on the schedule of the temple.All are welcome to join us.

Location
Cambridge Zen Center
More Details

The Black, Indigenous and People of Color (BIPOC) Sangha is a monthly gathering of self-identifying BIPOC practitioners that provides a safe place to meditate and explore contemplative practices. This sangha cultivates an environment that welcomes diverse voices and builds community. In a typical practice group, we enjoy a mix of walking meditation, sitting meditation, chanting, readings, and dharma sharing about our experiences and practice in everyday life.
