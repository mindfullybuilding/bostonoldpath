Join our mailing lists

Choose the messages you would like to receive. Our mailing lists are managed through Groups.io; the newsletter archive is available separately.

Practice announcements

Sitting updates, retreats, and community announcements through the Old Path list.

Request a practice-list subscription

oldpath+subscribe@BostonOldPath.groups.io

Member sharing

Member-to-member sharing through the separate Member Sharing list.

Request a member-sharing subscription

membersharing+subscribe@BostonOldPath.groups.io

How to subscribe

  1. Use the appropriate button to open a new email, or copy the address into your email app.
  2. Send the subscription request from the email address you want to use.
  3. Look for a confirmation email from Groups.io and follow its instructions. Check spam if it does not arrive.

For both lists, send a separate request to each address. A button click alone does not complete a subscription.

Manage your subscription

Use the subscription preferences or unsubscribe link in a list email. For assistance, contact plumvillageboston@gmail.com.
