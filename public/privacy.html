Privacy and External Services

This site provides information about MCGB practice communities and links to services maintained by others.

Calendar, Maps, and Contributions

The embedded Google Map connects to Google’s services. The contribution form is hosted by Google Forms. Visiting or using these services is subject to their own privacy terms. The group directory provides a first-party alternative to browsing the map. The website calendar reads the public Groups.io feed.

Mailing Lists

Subscriptions are managed by Groups.io. Follow the confirmation and preferences instructions from that service. We do not enroll you by merely visiting this website.

Contact and Corrections

For site corrections, privacy questions, press inquiries, or accessibility feedback, email plumvillageboston@gmail.com.

Community Information

Group details can change. Contact the organizer to confirm a meeting before attending. Historical documents reflect their original publication dates.
