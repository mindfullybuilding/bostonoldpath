Access to practice

Accessibility statement.

We aim to make this website usable with keyboards, screen readers, magnification, and a wide range of screen sizes.

Scope and assessment

This redesign targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Release verification covers the first-party HTML pages, navigation, group directory, calendar agenda, and subscription guidance. Assessment date: 19 October 2026. This is an internal verification effort, not an independent accessibility certificate.

Known limitations

  • Historical newsletter PDFs and original documents have not been comprehensively remediated.
  • The optional Google Calendar view requires sign-in. Google Calendar, Google Maps, Google Forms, and linked websites have their own accessibility behavior. Their complete processes are not certified by this statement.
  • The accessible event agenda and group directory remain available independently of the embedded calendar and map.
  • Screen-reader coverage and open findings are recorded in the release verification report; untested combinations are not claimed to pass.

Feedback and assistance

Email plumvillageboston@gmail.com with the page address, what you were trying to do, and any browser or assistive technology details you wish to share. You may also request help accessing a newsletter article, submitting a contribution, or finding organizer contact information.
