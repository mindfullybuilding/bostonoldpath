Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston is an interfaith collective that seeks to learn and practice mindfulness taught by Thích Nhất Hạnh (“Thầy”), a Vietnamese Buddhist monk, author and activist.

Find a GroupNew? Start here.

Practice & community

We explore ways to bring mindfulness into our lives, families, work, and society. We try to practice mindfulness throughout every day, not just during meditation, but also in the kitchen, in the car, at work, on the phone, and while walking from one place to another.
