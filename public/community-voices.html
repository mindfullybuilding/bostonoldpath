Community Voices

A place for work that grows from mindfulness and community. Our newsletter archive offers a starting point; new contributions are reviewed before publication.

Offer your voice.

Share a link to your art, writing, song, photograph, or other contribution, along with a title and description. The contribution form also asks about collaborators.

Please share only work you have permission to submit. A person reviews submissions; completing the form does not publish your work automatically.

Submit your contribution

Opens a Google Form. This temporary form accepts links and descriptions, rather than direct file uploads.

Need another way to contribute?

If the form is difficult to use, contact plumvillageboston@gmail.com to discuss a contribution or request help.
