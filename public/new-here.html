You may be curious about meditation, returning to practice, or looking for people to practice with. You can begin by learning about a group and contacting its organizer.

What is a sangha?

Sangha means a community of practice. In our tradition, friends support one another in cultivating mindfulness, understanding, and compassion.

Choosing a group

Compare location, meeting format, and schedule. Some groups have a particular focus or community; read the details before joining.

Confirm the next meeting, joining instructions, registration or cost, and any access arrangements with that group’s organizer.

What might a gathering include?

Groups may practice sitting meditation, walking meditation, readings, mindful listening, and Dharma sharing — reflecting together on life and practice. The format and length vary by group.

Access and participation

Ask the organizer about chairs, movement, restrooms, stairs, sensory needs, online captions, or anything else that would help you participate. The directory preserves organizer-provided access information; it is not a verified accessibility survey of venues.
