Mindfulness woven into ordinary life.

Begin where you are

Practice is not separate from washing dishes, driving, listening, or taking a morning shower. The training is to bring body and mind back together in the middle of daily life.

Practice with sangha

Community gives the path warmth and steadiness. Each person becomes a reminder to pause, soften, and begin again.

Daily awareness

Mindfulness is practiced in ordinary moments, not reserved for a meditation cushion or a special room.

Communal rhythm

The sangha makes practice more relaxed, joyful, and durable by offering companionship along the path.

Plum Village roots

The forms are simple and concrete: sitting, walking, meals, study, song, and mindful action.

A hypothetical timeline

From living-room practice to a wider path.

This mock timeline sketches how a mindfulness community might grow from small gatherings into a shared regional rhythm.

1990

Seeds of practice

A few practitioners begin meeting informally, bringing sitting meditation into homes and borrowed rooms.

1994

Weekly rhythm

The group experiments with a steady weekly evening: sit, walk, read aloud, and share tea.

1998

Mindful meals

Potluck meals become practice, with silence, gratitude, and conversation held as one continuous form.

2001

Days of mindfulness

Longer Saturdays offer walking meditation, dharma study, and space for newcomers to learn the basics.

2005

Regional connection

Nearby groups begin sharing calendars, teachers, songs, and practical support across the Greater Boston area.

2009

New facilitators

Longtime members mentor newer facilitators so local sanghas can remain warm, steady, and welcoming.

2013

Dharma study circles

Small groups explore books, talks, and everyday practice questions with a beginner-friendly tone.

2017

Community care

The sangha experiments with clearer hospitality, newcomer orientation, and shared volunteer roles.

2020

Online doorway

Remote practice becomes a lifeline, helping people keep a weekly rhythm even when rooms are unavailable.

2023

Hybrid sangha

In-person and online gatherings are treated as connected doors into the same practice community.

Our history

From simple forms

The community story begins with accessible practice forms: breathing, sitting, walking, and returning to the present moment. The forms are intentionally plain so newcomers can participate without needing special background.

Into shared responsibility

Over time, the work expands from showing up to caring for the container. Members learn how to welcome, facilitate, prepare rooms, hold silence, and keep the calendar alive.

Held by everyday life

The practice keeps returning to ordinary actions. A walk, a meal, a difficult email, or a moment of listening can become a bell of mindfulness.
