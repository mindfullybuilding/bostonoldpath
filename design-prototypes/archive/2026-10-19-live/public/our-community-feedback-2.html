Practice & Community / Our Community

A sangha rooted in everyday mindfulness.

We are Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston, a community of local practice groups in the Plum Village tradition of Thích Nhất Hạnh. We welcome people who are new to mindfulness and those with an established practice.

Many local circles

Our groups meet in person, online, and in hybrid gatherings across Greater Boston. Shared events bring these local circles together. Learn about our Plum Village roots and explore community resources.

A sangha is a practice community. We support one another in returning to mindfulness with warmth and steadiness. Find a group.

Draft: a hypothetical timeline

From small circles to a regional sangha.

This mock timeline sketches how a Greater Boston mindfulness community might grow while keeping local sanghas at the center.

1990

First shared sits

A small circle of practitioners begins gathering around sitting, walking, mindful speech, and tea.

1994

Home sanghas

Practice finds a steady rhythm in living rooms, kitchens, and neighborhood spaces across Greater Boston.

1998

A shared community

A shared identity gives the wider community a memorable shorthand and shared sense of belonging.

2001

More local doors

Additional sanghas make it easier for newcomers to find a practice group close to their daily lives.

2005

Regional calendar

Sanghas begin coordinating shared events, days of mindfulness, study circles, and seasonal gatherings.

2009

Outdoor practice

Walking meditation and informal outdoor gatherings add a spacious way to practice together.

2013

Community builders

Facilitators and volunteers clarify how to welcome people, host rooms, and keep the wider network connected.

2017

Shared teachings

Study and reflection across sanghas strengthen the community around Plum Village practice in daily life.

2020

Online sangha

Online meetings keep practice accessible and make the community easier to reach from different places.

2026

Temple gathering

A Day of Mindfulness at Chua Viet Nam temple in Roslindale models a warm cross-sangha gathering.

Now

Belonging made visible

The next chapter makes the map, calendar, and pathways into community easier for new practitioners to understand.

Practicing together

Our community grows through sitting together, listening deeply, and caring for one another. Each gathering is an opportunity to begin again.

The timeline above is an illustrative draft. Dates and milestones require community verification before publication.
