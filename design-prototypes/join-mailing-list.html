Mailing lists

Our lists use Groups.io, so joining takes an email and a confirmation step. An email app is optional.

  1. 1. Choose Select the list or lists that fit what you want to receive.
  2. 2. Send Send a blank email to each selected address. In browser-based email, compose a new message and paste the copied address into To. Use the email account where you want to receive updates.
  3. 3. Confirm Follow the confirmation instructions sent by Groups.io. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive.
