Our Community

Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston (MCGB) is an association of mindfulness meditation groups, called sanghas, that practice in the tradition of Plum Village, founded by Vietnamese Zen Master Thích Nhất Hạnh. This tradition includes studying and practicing Buddhist teachings, called the Dharma; gathering as sanghas; and cultivating our inherent capacity for presence of mind and compassion for all beings—our Buddha nature.

Individual sanghas meet regularly and practice in ways that fit their members. They also gather from time to time for events such as peaceful walks, recitations and ceremonies of the 5 Mindfulness Trainings (Buddhist ethical teachings), daylong retreats, and groups such as the book club.

The Boston region has a long history of community practice, including many visits from Thích Nhất Hạnh, long-established sanghas, and numerous members of the Order of Interbeing (an order of ordained lay individuals). Learn more under Resources.
