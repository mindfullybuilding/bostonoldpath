Our Community

Mindfulness Communities of Greater Boston (MCGB) is an association of mindfulness meditation groups, called sanghas, that practice in the Plum Village tradition of Vietnamese Zen Master Thích Nhất Hạnh (Thay). Our tradition includes studying and practicing Buddhist teachings (the Dharma), and gathering as sanghas. We cultivate our inherent capacity for presence of mind and compassion for all beings—our Buddha nature.

Individual sanghas meet regularly and practice in ways that fit our members. We also gather from time to time for events such as peaceful walks, recitations and ceremonies of the 5 Mindfulness Trainings (ethical teachings), daylong retreats, and groups such as a book club.

The Boston region has a long history of community practice, including many visits from Thích Nhất Hạnh and his monastic community, long-established sanghas, and numerous members of the Order of Interbeing (an order of ordained lay individuals). Learn more under Resources.
