Accessibility Statement

We aim to make this website usable with keyboards, screen readers, magnification, and a wide range of screen sizes.

Scope and Assessment

This redesign targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Release verification covers the first-party HTML pages, navigation, group directory, calendar agenda, and subscription guidance. Assessment date: 20 October 2026. This is an internal verification effort, not an independent accessibility certificate.

Known Limitations

  • Historical newsletter PDFs and original documents have not been comprehensively remediated.
  • Google Maps, Google Forms, and linked websites have their own accessibility behavior. Their complete processes are not certified by this statement.
  • The map shows venues with a confirmed public location. The full group directory, schedules, and organizer contacts are available independently of the map.
  • The public calendar and agenda use the Groups.io feed without requiring a Google account.
  • Screen-reader coverage and open findings are recorded in the release verification report; untested combinations are not claimed to pass.
