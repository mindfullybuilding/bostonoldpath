Our practice

We explore ways to bring mindfulness into our lives, families, work, and society.

We try to practice mindfulness throughout every day, not just during meditation, but also in the kitchen, in the car, at work, on the phone, and while walking from one place to another.

Mindfulness

Mindfulness practice develops the power of our mind to be aware of what is happening, while it’s happening, without judgment and without interference. It’s simple, but not easy. We learn to observe and to develop curiosity as we practice bringing our full presence to whatever we do.

Sitting meditation

The basic practice is to find a comfortable upright position, focus our attention on our breath, and calm our chattering minds.

As our minds become more stable, we can follow our breath, giving it our full attention, noticing the changing sensations as we breathe in and as we breathe out. When our attention drifts from our breathing, we simply note that we have drifted and without judgment or self-criticism return our attention to our breathing.

Walking meditation

During walking, we coordinate our steps with our breathing, walking as a community. As we breathe in, we take a step; as we breathe out, we take a step, mindful of our breath and our steps. Our bodies are upright and relaxed, our eyes softly focused just ahead of us. When our mind drifts to the past or to the future, we gently guide it back to the present moment.

Dharma sharing

We share from our hearts. We say what is true for us, what really matters. It may be helpful to imagine the words literally emerging from our hearts, from our center of being. We do not advise, fix, explain, respond to or correct others.

Practicing with a group

A sangha is a community of Buddhist practitioners. Practicing together offers support and shared understanding, and helps us sustain mindfulness in daily life.

Joining a local sangha brings us into the collective practice of mindfulness, peace, and joy. Our sangha directory lists Greater Boston groups and their organizer contacts.
